Fish Piccata

Description

Fish piccata is that rare dish where elegance and efficiency intersect. The most important factor in this satisfyingly bright, buttery, briny dish is tender, juicy white fish. To achieve this, cook your breaded fillets almost entirely on one side. This allows for flavor-enhancing browning without overcooking the fish. Then all that’s left is to focus on emulsifying butter, wine, lemon juice, and capers into a quick, balanced pan sauce.
