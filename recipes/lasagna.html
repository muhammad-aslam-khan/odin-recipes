Lasagna

Description

Lasagna consists of flat pasta noodles layered with tomato or meat sauce, and a rich cheese blend of ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan. Traditional variations can also use a creamy béchamel white sauce instead of ricotta

Ingredients

  • Pasta: Flat, wide lasagna noodles (dried or fresh)
  • Sauce: Tomato sauce, crushed tomatoes, or a meat ragù with beef and Italian sausage
  • Cheeses: Ricotta (or cottage cheese), shredded mozzarella, and grated Parmesan
  • Aromatics & Seasonings: Garlic, onion, basil, oregano, parsley, salt, and black pepper
  • Binders: Eggs mixed into the ricotta to help the cheese layer set
