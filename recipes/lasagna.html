Beef Lasagna

Description

Lasagna consists of flat pasta noodles layered with tomato or meat sauce, and a rich cheese blend of ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan. Traditional variations can also use a creamy béchamel white sauce instead of ricotta

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef/minced beef
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup water
  • 9–12 lasagna sheets

Steps

  1. Make the meat sauce: Heat oil and sauté onion for 3–4 minutes. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  2. Add minced beef and cook until browned.
  3. Add tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt and pepper.
  4. Add water, reduce heat and simmer for 20–25 minutes.
  5. Make the white sauce: Melt butter, add flour and cook for about 1 minute.
  6. Gradually whisk in milk. Cook until the sauce thickens.
  7. Add Parmesan, salt and pepper.
  8. Assemble: Spread a little meat sauce in a baking dish.
  9. Add lasagna sheets, meat sauce, white sauce and mozzarella.
  10. Repeat the layers. Finish with white sauce and plenty of mozzarella.
  11. Bake at 180°C for 35–45 minutes, until bubbling and golden.
  12. Rest for 10–15 minutes before cutting.
