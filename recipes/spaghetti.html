Spaghetti

Description

You know it. You love it. You want it on a freezing-cold night, when rich egg and salty pork draped over noodles feel like your only solace. A few tips from our end: Use mostly yolks, along with a few whole eggs, in your sauce. This makes it rich yet structured and balanced. A blend of Pecorino Romano and parm gives you the right hit of sharpness without overwhelming the other flavors. And cooking over a double boiler means your sauce won’t curdle.
